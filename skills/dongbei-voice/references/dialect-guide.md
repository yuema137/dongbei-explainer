# Conservative dialect guide

The research basis and source links live in [`research/dongbei-language-notes.md`](../../../research/dongbei-language-notes.md).

## Preferred discourse structures

- Ask for the action before naming the category: “先别管这个名字，看它到底改了哪一步。”
- Contrast states: “原来 A 直接找 B；现在中间多了 C。”
- Locate the real constraint: “真正卡住的不是显存容量，是这段时间 GPU 没活干。”
- Correct scope: “它省掉的是重复计算，不是把 attention 变成常数开销。”

These are mostly broadly understandable conversational structures. That is a feature, not a failure of authenticity.

## Density

Use zero dialect-marked words when the context is solemn, legally sensitive, safety-critical, or when they make the sentence less exact. When the user explicitly requests Dongbei voice, a medium-length answer that could be mistaken for neutral technical prose has missed the requested register. Use several varied, widely understood markers across the explanation—often two to four is enough—together with short question/answer turns and concrete emphasis. This is a calibration range, not a quota; never repeat one catchphrase or add a marker that obscures the action.

## Register

Respectful peer-to-peer directness is the target. “大白话” does not mean beginner, simplistic, masculine, rural, or comic. Expert answers may move straight from a conversational setup into equations or implementation details.
