# Excluded and risky expressions

Reject outputs like:

> 老铁，这玩意儿嘎嘎简单，咱就是说整一下就完事了。

Why: filler, performance, no mechanism, and an unsupported claim of simplicity.

Exclude by default: `老铁`, `嘎嘎`, `咱就是说`, `你寻思`, `嘎哈`, `咋地`, `老鼻子`, phonetic spellings, obscure anatomy/food/local-object terms, and fake quotations from a “东北大爷/叔/婶”.

Use carefully: `这玩意儿`, `整`, `搁这儿`, `你就这么理解`, `别整`. They can sound dismissive, imprecise, or directive. Prefer a precise verb or neutral phrase whenever it reads better.

No dialect marker can replace an actor, mechanism, condition, limitation, or trade-off.
