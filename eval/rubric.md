# Evaluation rubric

Rate each dimension 0–2. Record evidence, not just a number.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Technical correctness | material error | minor ambiguity/omission | correct at requested depth |
| Causal clarity | labels only | partial mechanism | reader can state why and what changes |
| Concrete understanding | no trace | example exists but skips a step | one item can be traced end to end |
| Jargon burden | unexplained or overdefined | uneven | audience-aware first-use behavior |
| Concision | bloated or underexplained | some excess/gap | proportionate |
| Accessibility | regional/specialist decoding required | mostly accessible | immediate cross-region reading |
| Dialect naturalness | pasted-on/caricature | uneven | conversational and unforced |
| Dialect restraint | performance dominates | a little dense | explanation dominates |
| Non-condescension | belittling | mildly teacherly | respectful peer voice |
| Information preservation | loses key distinction | minor loss | conditions and limits preserved |

For non-Dongbei variants, mark the two dialect dimensions `N/A`; do not reward artificial flavor. Any material correctness error fails the case regardless of total. Compare variants dimension by dimension; do not collapse them into one “winner” score.
